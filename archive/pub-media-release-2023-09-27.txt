[Body, paragraph 5]

"The current potable water price is set at $2.74 per cubic metre. To meet the
higher costs of supplying and producing water, the potable water price will
increase by 50 cents per cubic metre (equivalent to 1,000 litres of water).
This will be implemented in two phases – an increase of 20 cents per cubic
metre from 1 April 2024, and a further 30 cents per cubic metre from 1 April
2025."

WHAT THIS DOCUMENT ESTABLISHES
------------------------------
1. The domestic water tariff from 1 April 2025: 1.43 below 40 m³/month, 1.81
   above. Tier 1, from the publisher.
2. The Waterborne Tax from the same date: 1.09 below 40 m³, 1.40 above.
3. The Water Conservation Tax rule: 50% of tariff below 40 m³, 65% above.

WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------
The rounding stage. The Annex A row is labelled "(% of Tariff)" and prints
0.72, while the stated rule gives 50% of 1.43 = 0.715. Under source precedence
a statutory or official formula outranks a rounded presentation table, so the
formula governs and the stored rate is 0.715. Whether SP Services bills 0.715
or 0.72 is a fact about billing, and only an invoice shows it. An invoice may
never supply a rate; it validates. The unresolved rounding stage is what holds
this city at Grade B rather than A: U = 0.233%.

It also does not establish that these rates are still in force. This release
announces a future revision. Currency is evidenced separately by the SP
Services tariff page (source id sp-tariff-information), SP being PUB's billing
agent.
