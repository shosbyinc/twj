WHAT THE PAGE STATES IN TEXT
  There are three components to the water price in the monthly bill: the Water
  Tariff, the Water Conservation Tax and the Waterborne Tax.
  The Water Conservation Tax is imposed as a percentage of the water tariff.
  The Waterborne Tax is a contribution to the national used water system and
  goes towards treating used water and maintaining the used water network.
  SP Services is PUB's billing agent.

WHAT THE PAGE DOES NOT STATE IN TEXT
  The rate table is published as an image file
  (/-/media/Notices/2025-water-price/PW-price.PNG) and contains no
  machine-readable figures. The rates of S$1.43 per cubic metre, the 50%
  conservation tax and S$1.09 waterborne tax are therefore NOT established by
  this capture.

SEPARATELY ESTABLISHED
  PUB media release, 2023: the Water Conservation Tax is applied at 50% of the
  tariff for domestic users consuming less than 40 cubic metres per month, and
  65% above that. All figures stated before GST.
