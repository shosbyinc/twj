[Perth metropolitan residential water use pricing 2026-27]

  Usage (kL)/bill year        $/kL
  Tier 1: 0 - 150 kL          $2.108/kL
  Tier 2: 151 - 500 kL        $2.809/kL
  Tier 3: over 500 kL         $5.256/kL

"From 1 July 2026, water use charges have increased by 2.75%."

[How the tiers accumulate — the reason this is not a monthly block tariff]

"At the beginning of your bill year, your water account starts in Tier 1, where
 you are charged the lowest price per kL for your location.

 A bill year is made up of 6 two-monthly billing periods. It starts with the
 first meter reading of your reading cycle, which can be any time from March to
 October, depending on where you live.

 Throughout a bill year, we keep track of your total water use since it started.
 As your water use goes up, you'll continue moving closer to the next highest
 tier until your bill year ends.

 If you have moved up a tier during your bill year, you will revert to Tier 1
 when your new bill year starts."

"For the bill you receive in July or August, your water use charges will be
 priced at the previous financial year's tier prices. Prices are updated for
 each financial year."

[2026-27 residential service charges]

  Service charge                Perth metropolitan area
  Water service charge          $305.05

"Residential water service charges are a fixed, state-wide charge which is
 reviewed and set each year by the State Government.

 All properties that have a Water Corporation water service available to it
 (including those not currently connected) are billed for this charge."

"Your service charges are divided across 6 two-monthly bills for the year, so
 the amount you pay on each bill depends on the number of days within that
 billing period."

"From 1 July 2026, water, sewerage and drainage service charges have increased
 by 2.75%."

[Water Corporation's own worked example — a public reconciliation]

"For this example, the sewerage charge of $917.70, drainage server charge of
 $142.86 and water service charge of $305.05 totals $1,365.61 for the year."

  917.70 + 142.86 + 305.05 = 1,365.61  ✓

[Sewerage and drainage — why they cannot be standardised]

"Residential sewerage and drainage charges are based on your property's Gross
 Rental Value (GRV), multiplied by a rate in the dollar."

"The use of GRV to calculate your charges is set by law, as it's deemed to be
 the fairest way to do so for properties throughout WA."

  Sewerage: 3.059 cents in $ GRV to $30,000; 3.946 cents above.
            Minimum annual charge $506.73.
  Drainage: 0.444 cents in $ GRV. Minimum annual charge $142.86.

[Residential water service charge does not vary by meter size]

The Business tab prices the water service charge by meter size — $324.18 up to
20 mm, $506.56 at 25 mm, and so on up to $99,282.40 at 350 mm. The Household tab
carries one figure, $305.05, described as fixed and state-wide. The distinction
is the publisher's own, and it is what makes the residential reference
connection size-independent while the business one is not.

WHAT THESE DOCUMENTS ESTABLISH
------------------------------
1. Perth metropolitan residential water use tiers for 2026-27: $2.108/kL to
   150 kL, $2.809/kL to 500 kL, $5.256/kL above, assessed over a bill year.
2. That the tiers accumulate across six two-monthly billing periods and reset at
   the start of each bill year. The threshold is crossed once a year, not once
   per bill.
3. The residential water service charge: $305.05 a year, fixed state-wide, set
   by the State Government, billed to every property with a service available.
4. That residential sewerage and drainage are assessed on Gross Rental Value by
   law, and therefore cannot be standardised for a comparative bill.
5. That the residential water service charge does not vary by connection size,
   while the business equivalent does.

WHAT THEY DO NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------
The GST treatment. That is a matter of Commonwealth statute, not of a utility
page: see source antsa-gst-act-38-285.

The bill year start date for any particular property. It is the first meter
reading of the reading cycle, which falls between March and October depending on
location. The benchmark therefore prices a full bill year at 2026-27 rates,
which no individual customer experiences exactly, because a real bill year
straddles a price change on 1 July. Recorded as scope.
