WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
---------------------
Greater Sydney storage stands at 92% as published by the storage authority, with
a weekly change of -0.1%. Sydney Water's retail tariff engages its drought usage
rate when Greater Sydney storage falls BELOW 60% and disengages once it is above
70%. At 92% the tariff state is normal and the published usage rate of
A$3.41/kL applies.

WHY THE UNRESOLVED TRIGGER MECHANICS DO NOT BITE HERE
-----------------------------------------------------
The Sydney record has carried an open question about the exact trigger
mechanics, because WaterNSW's wholesale determination defines a 31-day lag at
Part 7 Clause 25 while Sydney Water's retail page states the thresholds without
one, and the two instruments must not be conflated.

That question decides the applicable rate only near a crossing. At 92% the
storage sits 32 points above the engagement threshold and has moved 0.1 point in
a week; no lag of any length — 31 days or none — selects the drought rate from
this level. The mechanics remain unresolved and are reclassified from a material
blocker to a validation gap, because the number does not depend on the answer at
this state.

If Greater Sydney storage approaches 60%, the classification reverses and the
mechanics become material again. That is recorded on the observation so the
reclassification is not silently inherited by a future reading.

WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------
An exact reading date. WaterNSW publishes a current level with a weekly change
rather than a timestamped reading, so observed_at is the capture date and the
figure is a weekly-updated series. A historical state for a past benchmark date
would need the storage history series rather than this front-page figure.
