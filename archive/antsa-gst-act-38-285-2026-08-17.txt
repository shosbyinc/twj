VERBATIM EXTRACT
----------------

s 38-285(1):  "A supply of water is GST-free."

Related, recorded for the wastewater side:
  Subdivision 38-I makes GST-free a supply of water (s 38-285), sewerage and
  sewerage-like services (s 38-290), the emptying of a septic tank (s 38-295)
  and a service that consists of draining storm water (s 38-300).

Commissioner's ruling on scope, for reference and not as the operative source:
  ATO Goods and Services Tax Ruling GSTR 2000/25, "GST-free supplies of water,
  sewerage and sewerage-like services, storm water draining services and
  emptying of a septic tank."

WHAT THIS DOCUMENT ESTABLISHES
------------------------------
That a supply of water in Australia is GST-free by statute. Under source
precedence a statutory provision outranks a ruling, and both outrank a utility
page — so the zero-tax treatment of the Perth bill rests on the Act.

WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------
The treatment of every individual line on a water bill. GSTR 2000/25 states
that suppliers impose various fees and charges, that not all of them are
GST-free — plans of sewer pipe locations and photocopying are named as taxable —
and that the ruling does not cover Australian taxes or fees under Division 81.
Nothing modelled in the Perth record falls outside the supply of water: the
volumetric charge and the water service charge are both consideration for
making water available to the premises.
