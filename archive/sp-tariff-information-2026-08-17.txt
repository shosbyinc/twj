WHAT THIS DOCUMENT ESTABLISHES
------------------------------
That the domestic water tariff in force at capture is 1.43 per m³ below 40 m³
per month and 1.81 above, before GST — the same figures the PUB release set for
1 April 2025, unchanged as at Q3 2026.

WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------
The Water Conservation Tax and the Waterborne Tax. This page publishes only the
water tariff line. Those two components rest on the PUB release.

It shows a GST-inclusive figure of 1.56 / 1.97 against 1.43 / 1.81 before GST.
1.43 × 1.09 = 1.5587, presented as 1.56. Consistent with 9% GST, and evidence
about presentation rounding on the tariff line only — not about the Water
Conservation Tax, whose rounding stage remains the open question.
