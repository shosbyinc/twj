RECORDED AT CAPTURE
  1. The page states the slab in AED per cubic metre. The verified residential
     invoice bills in AED per imperial gallon at 0.035. At 3,740 IG the invoice
     charges 130.90; the per-m³ rate over the same volume gives 130.92. The
     per-gallon rate is the operative one and reproduces the bill exactly, so
     source precedence keeps it: statutory formula, then billing formula, then
     invoice rate, before a rounded presentation table.
  2. The surcharge shown is for AUGUST 2026. July 2026 is not evidenced by this
     capture. DEWA resets the surcharge monthly.
  3. The page does not state which meter type applies to a standard residential
     connection. Type 1 at AED 5 is established for a Residential – Flat account
     by the verified invoice, and remains a scope disclosure rather than a
     universal rule.
