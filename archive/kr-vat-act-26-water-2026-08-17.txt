VERBATIM EXTRACT — the provision relied upon
--------------------------------------------

  「부가가치세법 제26조【재화 또는 용역의 공급에 대한 면세】
   ① 다음 각 호의 재화 또는 용역의 공급에 대하여는 부가가치세를 면제한다.
   ...
   2. 수돗물」

  Article 26 (Exemption from VAT on the supply of goods or services)
  (1) VAT is exempted on the supply of the following goods or services:
      ... 2. tap water (수돗물)

[The definition, which is what makes the exemption apply to Arisu]

  「부가가치세법기본통칙 26-0-1 【수돗물의 정의】
   법 제26조제1항제2호에 규정하는 "수돗물"은 「수도법」상의 수도사업자가 도관에
   의하여 공급하는 물과 「수도법」상의 수도사업자에게 직접 공급하는 수도사업
   (공업용 수도사업 및 전용수도를 포함한다)용 물(원수)을 말한다.」

  Basic Ruling 26-0-1: "tap water" under art. 26(1)2 means water supplied
  through conduits by a water supplier under the Water Supply Act, and raw
  water supplied directly to such a supplier.

[A National Tax Service reply on scope, recorded because it names the limit]

  「수도법상 수도사업자가 아닌 사업자가 자기 책임과 계산하에 수돗물을 공급하는
   경우에는 부가가치세가 과세되는 것이나...」

  Where a person who is NOT a water supplier under the Water Supply Act
  supplies tap water on their own account, VAT is chargeable. The exemption
  attaches to the statutory supplier, not to the substance.

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
---------------------
That the supply of tap water by a statutory water supplier is exempt from
Korean VAT. Seoul Arisu is the water supplier for Seoul Special City under the
Water Supply Act, so its water usage charge and its meter basic charge — both
consideration for the supply of tap water — carry no VAT.

This closes the open component on the WATER SUPPLY metric. It was opened
because Arisu's rate table shows no tax line, and the absence of a line in a
rate table proves nothing: Tokyo's table shows no tax line either while its
calculation formula multiplies by 1.10. The answer is in tax law, which is
where it was always going to be.

WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------
THE SEWERAGE SIDE. Article 26(1)2 exempts 수돗물. It does not mention 하수도
사용료. A sewerage use charge is levied by a local authority under the Sewerage
Act rather than supplied by a business, which is a different question from
exemption and is not answered here. The total water services figure therefore
stays open.

THE WATER-USE BURDEN LEVY. 물이용부담금 is a statutory levy under river
management law, not consideration for a supply. Almost certainly outside VAT for
that reason, and "almost certainly" is not a source.

WHY THIS CAPTURE IS MARKED PARTIAL
----------------------------------
The article number and the basic ruling are quoted identically across a National
Tax Service reply and several professional commentaries, which is strong
corroboration of the text. It is not a capture of the statute. Closing the
sewerage question needs law.go.kr obtained by hand, or a Seoul Metropolitan
Government document stating the treatment of 하수도 사용료 directly.
