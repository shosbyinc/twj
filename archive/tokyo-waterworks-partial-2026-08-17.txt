VERBATIM EXTRACT — the Bureau's own text
----------------------------------------

[The billing formula, 23 wards]

  「【水道料金】 計算式（基本料金＋従量料金）×1.10
   （1円未満の端数は、切り捨てます。）」

  Water charge = (basic charge + volumetric charge) x 1.10, with fractions
  below one yen TRUNCATED, not rounded.

  「【下水道料金】 計算式 料金表に基づき算定した金額×1.10
   （1円未満の端数は、切り捨てます。）」

[The Bureau's worked bi-monthly example]

  「（基本料金 1,170円×2か月分 ＋ 従量料金 3,020円+2,857円）×1.10＝9,038円」

  (1,170 x 2 + 3,020 + 2,857) x 1.10 = 9,038.7, published as 9,038.
  Confirms the basic charge of 1,170 yen for the connection in the example, the
  bi-monthly billing cycle, the 10% rate, and truncation.

[The basic volume included in the basic charge]

  「一般家庭など口径２５ミリ以下のお客さまに対しては、月５m³までを基本水量として
   基本料金に含め、従量料金をいただいておりません。」

  For customers at 25 mm or below, up to 5 m3 per month is a "basic volume"
  INCLUDED IN THE BASIC CHARGE, and no volumetric charge is levied on it. This
  is not a free tier in the ordinary sense; it is an allowance attached to the
  basic charge.

[The Bureau's per-cubic-metre example]

  「例えば、口径２０ミリで１か月２４立方メートル使用した場合で計算しますと、
   水道料金は、１立方メートル当たり消費税込みで約１４７．２円になります。」

  20 mm, 24 m3 in one month: about 147.2 yen per m3 including consumption tax.

[The waiver page]

  「※使用水量に応じて加算される従量料金、及び下水道料金は無償の対象外です」
  「基本料金 無償期間は０円と記載されます
   ※通常は2か月分の料金が記載されます」

  The volumetric charge and the sewerage charge are OUTSIDE the waiver. During
  the waiver the basic charge line reads zero.

[The rate table page carries its own effective date]

  「23区用 令和元年12月分から適用」 — applied from the December 2019 billing month.

WHAT THIS PARTIAL CAPTURE ESTABLISHES
-------------------------------------
1. The billing formula and its rounding rule: (basic + volumetric) x 1.10,
   truncated to the yen. Stated twice, once for water and once for sewerage.
2. The basic charge of 1,170 yen a month for the connection in the Bureau's own
   example, and the 10% consumption tax rate, from the same worked example.
3. That 5 m3 a month is a basic volume inside the basic charge for connections
   of 25 mm or below — a structural point the calculation draft had modelled as
   an ordinary zero-rated first tier.
4. That the waiver covers the basic charge only, and that sewerage and the
   volumetric charge are billed as normal.
5. That billing is bi-monthly, so a tier threshold applies to a two-month
   volume, and that the rate table has been in force since December 2019.

THREE RECONCILIATIONS AGAINST THE BUREAU'S OWN FIGURES
------------------------------------------------------
Using the tier structure from the calculation draft — basic 1,170; volumetric
0 for the first 5 m3, 22 from 6 to 10, 128 from 11 to 20, 163 from 21 to 30 —
the engine reproduces every figure the Bureau publishes:

  a) 20 mm, 24 m3 in a month:
     volumetric 2,042 + basic 1,170 = 3,212; x 1.10 = 3,533.2, truncated 3,533;
     3,533 / 24 = 147.2 yen per m3.
     The Bureau says "about 147.2 yen". EXACT.

  b) The bi-monthly worked example above: 9,038.7 truncated to 9,038. EXACT.

  c) The waiver's own value for a 20 mm connection over four months, reported by
     the Bureau and in the press as 5,148 yen including tax:
     1,170 x 1.10 x 4 = 5,148. EXACT.

Three independent figures, one tier structure. That is strong corroboration.

WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------
THE TIER TABLE ITSELF, which is the thing being corroborated. Reproducing three
published totals constrains the tiers tightly, and it is not the same as reading
them. A different structure that happens to sum identically at 24 m3 and at the
example volumes cannot be ruled out from arithmetic alone. Under §7.1 a rate
that has not been read from a tier 1 or 2 source may not produce a published
figure, so Tokyo stays unpriced until 早見表（2か月用）(23区) is captured.

THE SEWERAGE TABLE. The draft computes (560 + 7 x 110) x 1.10 = 1,463 at 15 m3
and reports that an official Tokyo example gives exactly that. Neither the table
nor the example has been captured here.

THE WAIVER INSTRUMENT. The Bureau's guidance page is captured in extract; the
press release of 15 May 2026 and the terms 「水道料金の基本料金の無償について
（令和8年夏季）」are not. Under §5.4 the payable/structural split turns entirely
on the instrument, so it must be the original.

WHAT THE PARTIAL CAPTURE CORRECTED IN THE DRAFT
-----------------------------------------------
The waiver period is not a single month. It runs four months as either May to
August or June to September, depending on whether the property's meter is read
in an even or an odd month. The draft modelled it as July 2026. The payable
price is therefore period-dependent in a way a single monthly figure cannot
express, and the record must carry the read-cycle as scope.

Musashino City is not served by the Bureau and is outside the waiver. Some
bulk-metered apartment buildings are also outside it. Both are scope.

The measure was also run in the summer of 2025, so a measure the issuer calls
「今年の夏に限った臨時的な特別措置」— a temporary special measure limited to this
summer — has now happened two years running. It is funded by a transfer from the
metropolitan general account rather than from water revenue, which is why the
underlying tariff is unchanged and the structural price is the tariff.
