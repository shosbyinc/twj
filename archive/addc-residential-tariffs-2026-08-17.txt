WHAT THIS DOCUMENT ESTABLISHES
------------------------------
1. The expat apartment water rates: AED 7.84 per m³ in the Green band and
   AED 10.41 in the Red, with a Green allowance of 0.7 m³ per day.
2. That the band allowance is DAILY, and that the dwelling type sets it.
   The standardized 180 m³ a year is 0.492823 m³ a day, entirely inside Green.
3. The separate UAE National tariff at AED 2.09 and AED 2.60, and that it is
   elective on one property. It is a concessionary class under §5.3 and is
   stored separately, never averaged with the general one.
4. That ADDC's own tariff page states charges are inclusive of VAT at 5%,
   effective from the introduction of VAT in the UAE on 1 January 2018.

WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH — AND THIS IS THE WHOLE PROBLEM
----------------------------------------------------------
WHETHER AED 7.84 CONTAINS THE VAT OR ATTRACTS IT.

The footnote can be read two ways and both are ordinary English:
  (a) the published rates already contain VAT, so 15 m³ costs AED 117.60; or
  (b) the amounts ADDC charges you will be VAT-inclusive — that is, VAT is
      added and the total you pay includes it — so 15 m³ costs AED 123.48.

The handoff that queued this city reported the opposite conflict: an official
bill explainer showing the water consumption subtotal before VAT with 5% added
against a tariff page saying charges are inclusive. This capture is that tariff
page, and it does not settle the question so much as state one side of it in a
sentence that is itself ambiguous.

    inclusive reading   AED 117.60 a month   AED 7.840 per m³
    exclusive reading   AED 123.48 a month   AED 8.232 per m³
    U = 4.878%

Under §5.2 that is Grade C, not Grade B: above the 1% ceiling a figure may be
shown as context or a range and may never enter a ranking. Abu Dhabi is
therefore NOT PRICED, and the reason is not a missing document — it is a
five-per-cent ambiguity in an official footnote about the headline number.

Precedence cannot rescue it. §7.5 ranks a statutory formula above a billing
formula above an invoice rate above a presentation table. Both readings here come
from the same publisher and neither is a formula. What settles it is arithmetic
from ADDC itself: the official bill calculator run at a known volume, or a real
residential invoice.

ALSO NOT ESTABLISHED
--------------------
A RECURRING METER OR SERVICE CHARGE. This page shows none for water. Secondary
sources describe a monthly meter service charge of AED 7.84 for expats and
AED 5.23 for UAE Nationals — figures attached to the electricity tariff in those
sources, and AED 7.84 is confusingly also the water rate per m³. Whether a water
account carries a recurring charge is unresolved. Dubai charges AED 5.00 a month
for a Type 1 meter, and assuming Abu Dhabi charges nothing because this page is
silent is what Rule 7.6 forbids.

WHETHER SEWERAGE IS INSIDE THE WATER RATE. Secondary sources state that Abu
Dhabi bundles sewerage into the tariff rather than billing it separately. If true
this is the Toronto case and no supply-only figure exists; if false, a wastewater
component is missing entirely. Neither has been established, so no Total Water
Services figure is computed.

THE YEAR. The page is titled "Rates And Tariffs 2025" and last updated 26 May
2025. Whether these are the rates in force in 2026 has not been established.

Recycled water at AED 1.70 is excluded: a separate non-potable supply for
irrigation, not the domestic drinking water service.
