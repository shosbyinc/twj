WHAT THIS DOCUMENT ESTABLISHES
------------------------------
1. The billing formula. The rate is printed to four decimal places and
   multiplied by the metered volume, and the line amount is rounded to cents.
   33.89 / 1.4300 = 23.6993 and 25.83 / 1.0900 = 23.6972 — one consumption of
   23.7 m3 reconciles both lines:
       1.43 x 23.7 = 33.891 -> 33.89
       1.09 x 23.7 = 25.833 -> 25.83
   TWJ's engine reproduces both amounts exactly at that volume. This is an
   independent public reconciliation of the same kind Dubai has through its
   verified invoice, obtained without any private document.

2. The rounding order for tax: GST is computed per individual item and the
   items summed — not applied once to a grand total.

3. That the water tariff, Water Conservation Tax and Waterborne Tax are the
   three PUB components of a domestic bill, collected by SP for PUB.

WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------
The Water Conservation Tax rate as billed. The WCT line's rate and amount are
not present in the text layer of this capture. At 23.7 m3 the two candidate
values give:

    rate 0.7150 (the official formula, 50% of 1.43)  ->  16.95
    rate 0.7200 (the figure printed in PUB Annex A)   ->  17.06

Neither appears in the extracted text. The rate column of this sample bill does
carry four decimal places, so the field is capable of expressing 0.7150 exactly.
A visual read of that one field in this PDF would close the question; the text
layer alone does not.
