VERBATIM EXTRACT — the passages relied upon
-------------------------------------------

[Domestic supplies — the complete tariff]

"Domestic consumers are usually billed for their water charges at 4-monthly
 intervals according to the meter readings taken. The charges payable are
 calculated on a tariff structure which consists of 4 tiers with progressively
 increasing prices.

 The following tariff structure is applicable to water consumed in billing
 periods which commence on or after 16 February 1995:
   * the first tier of 12 cubic metres is free of charge;
   * the second tier of 31 cubic metres is charged at $4.16 per cubic metre;
   * the third tier of 19 cubic metres is charged at $6.45 per cubic metre; and
   * the fourth tier for any consumption above the level of 62 cubic metres is
     charged at $9.05 per cubic metre."

[The statutory period, and proration]

"For the purpose of calculating water charges, a 4-month period is defined as
 121.64 days in the Waterworks Regulations. Since water meters will not be read
 at intervals of exactly 121.64 days, the volume covered by each tier is
 adjusted on a pro-rata basis according to the actual number of days in the
 period between two meter readings. As such, a consumer who uses the same amount
 of water as another one but over a longer period of time will receive a lower
 water bill."

[Calculation Example 1 — reproduced verbatim]

  Registered consumer: Mr. Chan
  Consumption period: from 1/8/1995 to 6/12/1995 (127 days)
  Water consumption: 66 cubic metres

  Tier    Consumption                Rate(HK$)   Charges(HK$)
  first   12x127/121.64 = 12.529      0.00          0.00
  second  31x127/121.64 = 32.366      4.16        134.64
  third   19x127/121.64 = 19.837      6.45        127.94
  fourth  remaining 1.268             9.05         11.47
  Total   66.000                                  274.05

[Calculation Example 2 — reproduced verbatim]

  Registered consumer: Mr. Lee
  Consumption period: from 1/8/1995 to 26/11/1995 (117 days)
  Water consumption: 66 cubic metres

  Tier    Consumption                Rate(HK$)   Charges(HK$)
  first   12x117/121.64 = 11.542      0.00          0.00
  second  31x117/121.64 = 29.817      4.16        124.03
  third   19x117/121.64 = 18.275      6.45        117.87
  fourth  remaining 6.366             9.05         57.61
  Total   66.000                                  299.51

  "Mr. Lee pays more than Mr. Chan because he consumed the same quantity of
   water in a shorter period (117 days) than Mr. Chan (127 days)."

[What else can appear on a bill — the enumeration]

"Please note that the above calculations are for reference only. Actual bills
 will be different from the amounts calculated because of the Sewage Charges,
 rounding of odd cents brought down from last water bills or carried forward to
 next bills and surcharges of 5% for overdue bills."

[Sewage charges — domestic]

"Domestic consumers are billed for their sewage charges at a 4-month interval.
 The sewage charges are calculated at $2.92 (with effect from 1.4.2017) per cubic
 metre of water consumed, with an exemption for the first 12 cubic metres for a
 4-month period, which is defined as 121.64 days in the Waterworks Regulations."

[Non-domestic concession — recorded because it was checked, and does not apply]

"...reducing water charge, sewage charge and trade effluent surcharge ('TES')
 (if applicable) for non-domestic accounts by 50%... These concession measures
 will be valid for one year from 15 January 2026 to 14 January 2027."

WHAT THIS DOCUMENT ESTABLISHES
------------------------------
1. The complete domestic fresh water tariff: four tiers, 12 m³ free, then 4.16,
   6.45 and 9.05 per m³, per four-month period, in force since 16 February 1995.
2. The domestic sewage charge: 2.92 per m³ with the same 12 m³ exemption, in
   force since 1 April 2017.
3. The statutory period of 121.64 days, from the Waterworks Regulations
   (Cap. 102A), and that tier volumes prorate to the actual reading interval.
4. THE ABSENCE OF ANY STANDING CHARGE. The page enumerates the whole of what a
   domestic consumer pays — four tiers of water, plus sewage — and separately
   enumerates the only other things that can appear on a bill: sewage charges,
   odd cents carried between bills, and a 5% surcharge on overdue bills. No
   meter rent, no service charge, no standing charge. This is what turns the
   fixed-charge question from an assumed zero into a confirmed absence.
5. THE BILLING FORMULA, twice over, from the publisher's own worked examples:
   the prorated tier volume is rounded to three decimals before multiplying,
   and the line amount is truncated to cents rather than rounded. Example 1's
   third tier of 19.837 m³ bills 127.94, not the 127.95 the unrounded 19.83722
   would give; example 2's fourth tier bills 57.61 from 6.366 × 9.05 = 57.6123.
   The TWJ engine reproduces both examples exactly.

WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------
That there is no tax. Hong Kong levies no general consumption tax, and no tax
line appears in this enumeration, but the absence of a GST is a fact about Hong
Kong law rather than about this page.

The 5% surcharge on overdue bills is a penalty for late payment, not part of the
cost of receiving water, and is excluded under §8 with the other penalties.
