Toronto Water operates 24/7 and is solely funded by rates and user fees. Your
utility bill payments support safe drinking water, wastewater treatment
services, updates to aging infrastructure, and address the impacts of extreme
storms.

--- Water Rates ---

Effective January 1, 2026, City Council approved a 3.75 per cent rate increase
for Block 1 water consumers and a 1.25 per cent rate increase for Block 2 water
consumers.

2026 Metric Rates
                                                Rate if paid on   Rate if paid
                                                or before due     after due date
                                                date
  Block 1 - all water consumption up to 5,000 m3  $4.8629 per m3   $5.1188 per m3
  Block 2 - consumption over 5,000 m3             $3.3219 per m3   $3.4967 per m3

Block 2 rates apply to businesses participating in the Industrial Water Rate
Program.
