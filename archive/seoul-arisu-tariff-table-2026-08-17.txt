WHAT THIS DOCUMENT ESTABLISHES
------------------------------
1. The basic charge by meter bore: 1,080 won a month at 15 mm, 3,000 at 20 mm,
   5,200 at 25 mm. The charge is a function of connection size.
2. The domestic water usage rate: 580 won per m³, a single rate with no bands,
   under Article 23 of the Seoul Water Supply Ordinance.
3. The domestic sewerage rate for 2026: 480 won per m³, single rate, under
   Article 23 of the Seoul Sewerage Use Ordinance — together with a published
   schedule rising to 560, 630, 700 and 770 won through 2030.
4. The water-use burden levy: 170 won per m³, universal across categories and
   in force since 1 January 2011. Mandatory and volumetric, which is what admits
   it as a water-specific levy rather than a discretionary charge.
5. The complete set of what Arisu bills a domestic customer: a basic charge, a
   usage charge, a sewerage charge and the levy.

AN INTERNAL INCONSISTENCY IN THE PAGE, NOTED
--------------------------------------------
The prose beneath the water table states 「사용요금은 4개업종 모두 3단계의
누진요금체계로 되어있고」— that all four categories use a three-band progressive
structure. The table above it shows domestic use as 1㎥당, a single rate, and the
same page dates the current table to 1 July 2021. The prose describes the
structure that preceded it and has not been updated. The table governs: a rate
schedule outranks a sentence describing the schedule.

WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------
THE TAX TREATMENT. No tax line appears anywhere in this table, and that is not
evidence of exemption. Tokyo is the counter-example: its rate table carries no
tax line either, while the Bureau's calculation formula multiplies by 1.10. A
rate table states rates; whether a tax is applied on top belongs to the
calculation method or to tax law. Korean domestic water is widely described as
VAT-exempt, and widely described is not sourced. Closing this needs either
Arisu's own 가정용 요금계산 formula page or the exemption provision of the Value
Added Tax Act.

THE REFERENCE CONNECTION. The table prices eight bores from 15 mm upward and does
not say which is the ordinary size for a single dwelling. Under §2.5 the
reference connection must be the size the utility explicitly designates, or
failing that the smallest size explicitly documented as suitable for an
individual dwelling. This table documents neither. Secondary sources describe
15 mm as the ordinary domestic bore; that is not a primary designation, so §2.5
bars Grade A until one is found.

APARTMENT ALLOCATION. Arisu's own moving-settlement page shows a domestic
apartment on a shared 15 mm meter with the basic charge apportioned between
households. A separately metered dwelling and an apportioned share of a bulk
meter are different reference customers.
