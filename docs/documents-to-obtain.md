Generated from `data/capture-manifest.json` on 2026-08-19.
Do not edit by hand: run `npm run list`.

For each document, what it must answer is printed beneath it. A capture that
does not answer its question has not closed anything.

**What a usable capture is:** the publisher's own file where possible, or the
text of the sections used from a direct fetch. A URL is not provenance —
every source is archived at collection and stored with a SHA-256 hash,
because a page updated next April stops proving this year's figure. Where the
publisher blocks automated fetching, a saved PDF or a print-to-PDF of the page
is worth more than any quantity of quoting.

*City of Toronto (Toronto Water)*  ·  wave 3

Queued for provenance and for a decision, not for a blocker. The tax component closed on 19 August 2026 and Total Water Services is published at Grade A. The supply metric is a structural exception under Rule 5.1 and is not waiting for a document at all.

> Whether the 2026 rate in the by-law is 4.8629 or 4.8630. The captured version is dated 11 February 2025 and gives C$4.6872; Council approved 3.75% from 1 January 2026, and 4.6872 x 1.0375 = 4.862970, which rounds to 4.8630 and truncates to 4.8629. The City's live rates page now prints 4.8629, which settles what the rate is and not how it was rounded — and the statutory schedule is what the record would roll to. Not a blocker: the 2025 observation is closed with a date and nothing published moves until somebody moves it.

**2. The Excise Tax Act provision behind the absent tax on a combined municipal water and wastewater charge**

> Why no tax is charged, which the bill shows but does not explain. Provenance rather than a figure: the City's worked bill closes on its two service charges, and that is what a household pays. The statutory route is not obvious, because relief for unbottled water supplied by a municipality and relief for wastewater services are separate questions with separate answers. Look in Schedule V, Part VI.

Traps:

- Do not accept 4.8629 from a secondary source because it agrees with the City's rounded total of C$1,118 at 230 m3. That total constrains the rate only to roughly 4.860-4.865.
- Schedule 3 Ref 2 prices a sewer surcharge on private water at 57% of Block 1, which invites a 43/57 decomposition into C$2.0155 supply. Refused under Rule 3.3 and recorded in the tariff as a refused inference. Do not revive it.
- The 2026 rates were approved as INTERIM and may be amended when the rate-supported budgets are considered. An interim rate is a different observation from a final one.
- The absence of a tax line and the absence of a mention are different evidence. A schedule that names HST against one fee and not another is suggestive; a worked bill whose total closes on its service charges is arithmetic. Do not cite the first as if it were the second.
- The City prints two rates, on-or-before and after due date, and the difference is the early payment discount. Applying the discount to the on-or-before rate counts it twice.
